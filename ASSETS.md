# Fixed means fixed.

<p class="lede">These are the canonical parent structures used by the project. They are references, not levels to be refitted to current price action.</p>

Child levels inside a smaller interval are always recalculated from the **exact neighbouring parent boundaries**.

> Current state can change. The ladder does not.


## Provenance

A fixed ladder and an ex-ante trading claim are not the same thing. The project records ladder freeze dates separately in **[PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md)**. Until a freeze date is established, earlier history is described as a fixed-ladder traversal study rather than an ex-ante backtest.
