# Fixed Structure Research

**Trade the structure. Understand the market.**

This repository is a small living research publication with two related but independently testable investigations:

1. **Trading method:** can a fixed logarithmic structural ladder be traded mechanically from adjacent level to adjacent level?
2. **Market understanding:** does the wider crypto market, especially TOTAL, reveal a repeatable structural rhythm when viewed through its 100-period simple moving average?

Start with **[the homepage](index.md)**, then read **[STRUCTURE](STRUCTURE.md)** before either branch.

> **Pan and zoom change the view, not the structure.**

New chart evidence belongs in [`charts/`](charts/) and new research notes belong in [`OBSERVATIONS.md`](OBSERVATIONS.md). Negative and unresolved examples are as welcome as striking ones.
